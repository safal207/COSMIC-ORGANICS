- equivalence to lattice QCD;
- quantum-computing behavior;
- FPGA board performance;
- post-route Fmax;
- real power or joules/op;
- ASIC PPA;
- superiority to CPUs, GPUs, TPUs, Cerebras or other accelerators;
- novelty or patentability;
- unique advantage from the names Cosmic Organic, MORPHOS, Bardo, Yin/Yang/Tao or ProofBit.
